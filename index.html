Our Security Services

Armed & Unarmed Security

Professional armed and unarmed security personnel for high-value assets, cash-in-transit, and VIP protection. Our trained teams ensure safe passage and maximum security.

  • Cash-in-transit protection
  • VIP & executive bodyguards
  • High-value asset escorts

Secure Transportation

Comprehensive secure transportation for valuable goods, documents, and personnel. Advanced tracking and communication systems included for full accountability.

  • GPS-tracked convoys
  • Sensitive document courier
  • Personnel safe transit

Risk Assessment & Planning

Professional risk assessment and strategic security planning. We identify potential threats and develop comprehensive security strategies tailored to your operations.

  • Threat vulnerability audits
  • Security protocol design
  • Emergency response plans

Patrol & Surveillance

24/7 patrol services and advanced surveillance systems to monitor and protect your premises, assets, and personnel around the clock without interruption.

  • On-site armed patrols
  • CCTV monitoring & control
  • Incident response teams

Access Control & Biometrics

State-of-the-art access control systems to regulate who enters your premises — from biometric scanners to smart card readers and visitor management.

  • Biometric entry systems
  • Visitor management
  • Perimeter control

Event Security

Professional security detail for corporate events, conferences, weddings, and luxury functions. Seamless crowd management and dignitary protection.

  • Corporate event security
  • Crowd control management
  • Luxury & private events
